# 04 — Grid Resilience Pivot

**Status:** Accepted doctrine (positioning thesis — not a VPP certification claim)  
**Audience:** Owners, developers, resilience planners

---

## Framing

Behind-the-meter and islandable assets are often treated as **parasitic load** during interconnection review: another thing that draws, complicates, or delays.

The same assets, commissioned with replayable proof, can be argued as **resilience and VPP-capable resources** — *if* state history and SAT outcomes are trustworthy. Without a ledger, “we can island” is a slide. With a ledger, it is a claim that can be inspected.

## What OIDF contributes

OIDF does not operate a virtual power plant. It makes commissioning truth portable across:

- equipment state machines (including energization clearance),
- handoff ledgers as digital as-builts,
- architecture packs for EV depot BTM, hyperscale island, and remote resilient microgrid.

Those packs document SAT sequences and safety-gate themes (NEC/NFPA-oriented checklists). They are **not** legal approvals. They are structured preparation for the bodies that issue approvals.

## Stance

Resilience is not a press release. It is a plant that can prove what state it reached, under what Evidence, including when the network was gone. OIDF is the format for that proof. Market programs and utility products sit on top — they do not replace the ledger.
