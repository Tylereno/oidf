# 03 — The Wedge Strategy

**Status:** Accepted direction (see also `docs/normative/0021-LIGHTHOUSE-PILOT.md`)

## Wedge

Drive adoption through a **narrow SAT / commissioning path** — not a platform for all critical infrastructure on day one.

| Dimension | First wedge |
|---|---|
| Asset | BESS / storage skid |
| Evidence | Machine telemetry (not form checkboxes) |
| Runtime | Keel lighthouse in `Tylereno/keel` |
| Success | `Installed → Commissioned` only after validated Evidence |

## Why SAT is the door

Site Acceptance Testing is where owners, EPCs, and AHJs already expect gates. OIDF turns those gates into **typed evidence + ledger events** instead of spreadsheet theater.

## Non-goals (wedge)

Fleet SaaS, OEM cert programs, replacing scheduling tools, VITO dashboard work.
